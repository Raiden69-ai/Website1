Statement No. 01 / Judgement
On hypocrisy, assumptions & consequences

The irony
of judgement.

It's ironic to be judged by people whose very own relationships render their judgement hypocritical.

If it's guilty until proven innocent, then just know I judge you the same way back from now on.

You think this ends with all of you, but the one who can end all this, is me.

Judgement cuts both ways.
Read carefully. Judge accordingly. END OF STATEMENT ■
